Sealed Bidding

We often use this for auctions but the idea is fairly general: getting responses from a group of people without them influencing each other. Name the participants and point everyone at the URL and Bob’s your uncle. Specifically, after everyone has responded aka bid, this page reveals everyone bids.

You could use it to get independent estimates of how long a project will take to implement. Or just use it to play Schelling’s coordination game. [1]

You’re free to replace your bid by just submitting again, which is allowed until the bids are revealed. When all the bids are in, the padlock starts glowing and the final revelation happens when someone clicks it.

Thanks to Bethany Soule and Christopher Moravec for making predecessors of this tool.
